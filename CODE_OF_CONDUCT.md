# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CtxDock a harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing impact, give actionable technical feedback, accept correction, and prioritize the community's well-being. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's available private maintainer contact mechanism. Maintainers will respect reporter privacy as far as practical and respond proportionately.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project.
